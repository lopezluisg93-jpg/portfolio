01
Problem

The Business Problem

HealthFlow's pay-per-visit model only works if patients return. Most didn't.

82%
The annual wellness gap
Patients need annual wellness exams to renew prescriptions, but 82% didn't proactively rebook. They'd lose medication access, get frustrated, and disappear.
89%
Bad experience churn
When a visit went poorly, 89% of those patients never returned. No win-back existed. No second chance was offered.
32%
One-size-fits-all follow-up
A patient treated for a UTI received the same follow-up as someone managing diabetes. The result: a 32% unsubscribe rate from messaging that didn't apply to them.
18%
State licensing friction
18% of bookings ended in cancellation because the chosen provider couldn't prescribe in the patient's state. The platform didn't prevent this — it just let it happen.
02
Strategy

Four interventions, each targeting a specific failure point

01
Annual wellness reactivation
A journey triggered 60 days before prescription expiration — not when it's already too late.
D-60: "Time to renew" → D-30: "Schedule now" → D-7: SMS urgency → Post-expiration: Recovery flow
02
Bad experience win-back
An NPS below 7 triggers a provider re-match sequence. Not an apology — a solution. Different doctor, better fit.
03
Acute vs. chronic segmentation
Classified at first visit. One-time conditions get a single relevant follow-up. Chronic conditions enter a recurring care journey with proactive check-ins.
04
Provider-state intelligence
Pre-booking validation surfaces only compatible providers. If the preferred doctor isn't available in the patient's state, alternatives appear automatically.
