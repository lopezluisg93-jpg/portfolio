01
Problem

The Business Problem

The issue wasn't acquisition. NovaPay had no shortage of signups. The problem was what happened after.

Where users dropped off after registration
Activated (first deposit / transaction)
29%
Verified identity, never added funds
12%
Opened app, abandoned KYC
21%
Never opened app after signup
38%
Trust deficit
New users had heard about digital wallet scams. The welcome flow offered no reassurance at the exact moment they were deciding whether to deposit money.
KYC abandonment
Identity verification felt opaque and burdensome. Users dropped mid-flow with no explanation of why it was required or how long it would take.
No reason to act today
The app communicated features. It never gave users a specific, personal reason to add money right now.
02
Strategy

Segment by barrier, not by demographics

Not all dormant users had the same problem. Each segment needed a different message, not a louder one.

Segment A
38%
Unengaged after signup
Registered, never opened the app. Lost motivation or forgot entirely.
Segment B
21%
Stalled in verification
Started KYC, didn't finish. Friction or distrust mid-flow.
Segment C

Verified but inactive
Completed identity check, never deposited. Trust — ready but uncertain.
Segment D
29%
Already activated
Excluded from this flow. Goes directly to onboarding & engagement lifecycle.
03
Execution

The 14-day activation Canvas

Day 0
Welcome + first hook
All segments
Leads with a tangible benefit based on acquisition source. $5 bonus anchors urgency from day one.
Push
"Welcome to NovaPay, [First Name]. Your $5 welcome bonus is waiting — make your first deposit to claim it."
Email
Welcome email with social proof stats, security badges, and "make your first deposit in 3 steps."
Day 2
KYC friction remover
Segment B only
Liquid logic detects exactly where the user stopped and addresses that specific moment. Not a generic reminder — a targeted nudge.
In-app
"You're 60% done. Tap to finish in under 2 minutes."
SMS
"Still need your ID? Here's what to have ready: front of ID + selfie. Takes 90 seconds."
Day 5
Trust sequence
Segments B + C
For users who've verified but haven't deposited, the barrier is almost always trust. Regulatory credentials, protection language, user testimonials.
Email
"Your money is protected. Here's how." — Full security explainer with regulatory logos and user testimonials.
Push
"NovaPay is regulated by [authority]. Your funds are insured. Start with as little as $10."
Day 10
Bonus expiry warning
All non-activated
Liquid personalizes by segment — unengaged users get social proof, skeptical users get "withdraw anytime, no lock-in."
Push
"Your $5 bonus expires in 4 days, [Name]. Don't leave it on the table."
SMS
"45,211 people joined this month. Your wallet is ready."
Day 14
Last attempt + sunset logic
All non-activated
Users who still haven't activated are tagged as cold leads and moved to low-frequency re-engagement. Protects deliverability, keeps the door open.
Email
"This is our last message about your bonus. Your account stays open — come back anytime."

Key learnings
01
Segment by barrier, not demographics
A 25-year-old and a 45-year-old who both completed KYC but never deposited have the same problem. Behavioral segmentation consistently outperforms demographic targeting.
02
Day 0 is the highest-leverage moment
Over 60% of activations happened within the first 48 hours. The welcome message had more impact than any urgency push later in the sequence.
03
Exit conditions are non-negotiable
Users who activated were immediately removed from the flow. Nothing damages trust faster than receiving a "make your first deposit" message after you already have.
